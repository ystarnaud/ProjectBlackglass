# Phase 12.5 manual test plan: combat presentation

**None of these checks has been performed.** The implementer worked in Unity batch mode (no audio device, no controller, no player build, a 640x480 window). Every row below is **not run** and is for the owner. Rendered still frames from batch mode exist in `.claude/scratchpad/phase125/renders/`; they are not a substitute for watching it play.

How the layer works and what each asset is: `Docs/CombatPresentation.md`. The decision: `Docs/Decisions.md` decision 044.

**Setup.** Two scenes:

- **ProceduralMission** (the Blind preset). It opens on the loadout panel; press `Deploy (new seed)`. Darius is the starting unit (slot 0, a Ranged rifleman with Aimed Shot and Blast); Sable carries Mend; Kestrel is the Marksman (Recon Scan). Keys: Space pause, Tab switch character, 1-4 abilities, Shift queue, X stop, R interact, I inventory, F1 developer overlay, F5 next fog preset (regenerates the same seed), F6 same seed, F7 new seed, F12 truth view (do **not** use F12 for the fog rows: the gate ignores it by design). Controller: see decision 024 and the Phase 11.5 sheet.
- **Presentation Test Scene** (`Assets/_Project/Scenes/Presentation Test Scene.unity`, press Play). An IMGUI panel on the left. Every button is described in `Docs/CombatPresentation.md`, "Preview scene". Use it for anything that needs repeating on demand.

The console must stay clean throughout (no exceptions; at most the loot-shortfall warning from Phase 12). The effects are placeholders and meant to be restrained: judge "readable and not obnoxious", not "finished".

## Automated results (separate from the manual checks above)

Automated coverage includes: the pure rules (visibility truth table and path sampling, budgets and quality scaling, envelopes, the cue throttle, voice picking, the sequence filter, cosmetic random), the profile and asset contents (no colliders on any effect prefab, scaled-time particle systems, the idempotent builder), the records (hit, cover miss, rejected attack, after exposure), duplicate prevention, the pooled-effect lifecycle, pause behaviour against the real generated assets (particles, tracers, recoil, flash, lights, camera impulse, audio voices), fog behaviour including dead victims, regeneration while running and paused, budgets under bursts and repeated explosions, presentation on versus off producing identical outcomes, the source audit (no `timeScale` writes, no unscaled time outside `CombatAudio`), the preview scene's wiring, and the `ProceduralMission` wiring.

Not covered by any automated test: how anything looks or sounds, a controller on hardware, a player build, the Prototype scene's feel, and frame rate. `PresentationPerformanceReportTests` is a measurement, not a gate; its numbers (`.claude/scratchpad/phase125/performance.txt`) come from batch mode at 640x480 and claim no frame-rate target.
