# Phase 11.5 manual test plan: the tactical HUD

Play `ProceduralMission` (it ships with the **Blind** preset; the HUD is on by default). Keys: F1 developer overlay, F5 next preset (regenerates the same seed), F6 same seed, F7 new seed, F9 operative panel, F12 truth view, M map overlay, Space pause, Tab / Shift+Tab switch character, F follow, R interact, 1-4 abilities, Shift (or LT) queue modifier. Controller: LB/RB switch character, D-pad up Follow, D-pad down Stop, RT + D-pad abilities, Start / Menu / Options / + pause. Layout is designed for a 1920x1080 canvas. The player HUD never shows debug text; the console must stay clean throughout (no exceptions, no warnings from the HUD).

1. **Start a mission.** Open `ProceduralMission`, press Play. The mission generates; no debug text is on screen. Expected: top-left objectives panel (header `MISSION ...`, rows), top-right extraction state and the FOLLOW and PAUSE chips, bottom-left squad cards, bottom-centre operative panel, bottom-right prompt list. The centre of the screen is empty.
2. **Developer overlay.** Press F1: the IMGUI debug text (unit labels, control hints, ability lines, mission debug panel) appears. Press F1 again: it disappears. Expected: hidden by default; with it hidden the HUD alone shows everything you need to play. The drag-select box (drag on the ground with the left button) is drawn in both states. F9, F12 and M still toggle their own views while F1 is off.
3. **Cycle operatives.** Press Tab, then Shift+Tab, through every operative (and LB/RB on a controller). Expected: the CONTROL marker moves to the controlled unit's card, the card order never changes (it is the Tab order), the operative panel shows the controlled unit's name, role, rank, health and abilities, and the selection follows control (SELECTED on the same card). Companions show one of FOLLOWING / ATTACHED / PARKED / HOLDING; the controlled unit shows none.
4. **Compare the squad cards.** With a mission running, look at all cards. Expected: each shows a portrait badge (initials plus role letter, a placeholder), display name (never an id), role, rank, a health bar and `current/max`, and the state words described above. A selected non-controlled unit reads SELECTED (outline), the controlled one CONTROL (filled).
5. **Take damage.** Let a hostile shoot a squad member. Expected: the card's health bar and number fall in step with the unit's health; the operative panel does the same when it is the controlled unit; the bar is drawn in a warning colour at low health.
6. **A unit goes down.** Let a squad member die. Expected: its card stays in place, dimmed, with `DOWN` and an empty bar; if it was controlled, control passes as with Tab and the CONTROL marker moves; the card does not disappear until the mission is regenerated.
7. **Abilities and cooldowns.** Tab to Kestrel. The operative panel shows ability slots, each with its prompt (`1`, `2` on keyboard; the pad chord on a controller), name and state. Use an ability. Expected: the slot shows the remaining seconds as a number (one decimal under 10 s, whole seconds from 10) over a fill that drains; `READY` returns at zero. A slot for an ability the unit does not have is hidden.
8. **Arming and the aiming line.** Press an ability key (or RT + D-pad). Expected: the slot reads `ARMED` with a bright frame; an `AIMING: <ability name>` line appears with, once the pointer is over a valid target, the target name (observed units only), `distance/range` and `OK`, `Moving into range`, or the failure wording; the prompt list shows Cast / Cancel / Queue. Press the key again or Cancel: disarmed, line gone. Aim at an unobserved enemy's spot: the line must not name it.
9. **Tactical pause.** Press Space (or Start / the PAUSE chip). Expected: a framed banner `TACTICAL PAUSE - <prompt> to resume` at top centre (the prompt follows your input device); the PAUSE chip reads RESUME; the prompt list switches to Resume / Order / Queue / Cancel / Switch; every cooldown number and fill is frozen; the queue panel can show up to 8 steps. Resume: banner gone, cooldowns run again.
10. **Queue several commands.** Paused, select a unit and queue with Shift (or LT): Move, Attack an observed hostile, Take cover (click a cover marker), Interact with a terminal in reach (R / Confirm), and an ability. Expected: the ORDERS list shows the current step with `> ` and the others numbered, in order: `Move`, `Attack <name>`, `Take cover`, `Interact <terminal>`, `<ability name>`; a long queue shows `+N`. In real time at most 3 steps show.
11. **Unknown target in the queue.** Queue an Attack on an observed hostile, then break its sight so it becomes a marker. Expected: the step reads `Attack (target lost)` and never the hostile's name.
12. **CLEAR.** Click CLEAR (mouse) or press Stop (D-pad down). Expected: the queue empties and the unit stops. A click on CLEAR does not also order anyone to move.
13. **Follow.** Press F (D-pad up) or click the `FOLLOW: ON/OFF` chip. Expected: the chip text flips; companion tags flip FOLLOWING <-> ATTACHED. Companions follow only when attached.
14. **Park and re-attach a companion.** Order a companion away (select it, click ground) so it is not following. Expected: its tag reads PARKED. Walk the controlled unit up to it (the magnet): the tag returns to FOLLOWING or ATTACHED. A companion holding at spawn, or holding ordered cover, reads HOLDING.
15. **Complete objectives.** Finish the terminal objective (hack it) and the elimination objective. Expected: each row's marker changes from `[ ]` to `[x]` and the text dims; a failed objective (if the mission can fail one) reads `[!]`, an objective not yet available `[-]`. The header shows the mission phase.
16. **Extraction.** Watch the top-right extraction chip across the mission. Expected, in order: `EXTRACTION LOCKED` (before objectives are done), `EXTRACTION AVAILABLE` (open, nobody inside), `EXTRACTION ACTIVE n/m IN ZONE` as squad members enter the zone, `EXTRACTED` and a SUCCESS result banner below the pause banner position when all are in. In the Blind preset it first reads `EXTRACTION UNKNOWN` until the extraction is found. Losing the whole squad shows the FAILED banner.
17. **Hidden objective.** In Blind, the objective panel lists vague lines such as `[?] Locate the data terminal`. Find the terminal's room. Expected: the vague line becomes the real description with a marker over the terminal; the elimination line shows kills, never a total.
18. **Observe an enemy.** Approach a hostile until it is seen. Expected: a small filled diamond above it, and the Target panel (above the prompts) with `HOSTILE`, its name, archetype, health bar and numbers and a tag (HOVERED when the mouse is on it, TARGETED for the soft target, ATTACKING when the controlled unit attacks it). With a ranged operative in control, the cover line (`Cover 65%` or `Exposed`) appears only while paused or when it is the attack target (it stays empty when the controlled unit does not need line of sight). The Target panel never blocks a click: clicking a hostile behind it still attacks it.
19. **Lose sight.** Break line of sight to the observed hostile. Expected: the filled diamond is replaced by a hollow diamond with `?` and `LAST KNOWN` at the last seen position; the Target panel closes; nothing about the hostile's health or movement updates. Walk back into sight: the live diamond returns and the hollow mark is gone.
20. **Unknown enemies never appear.** In Blind, with enemies you have not seen: nothing in the world marks, Target panel, queue, prompts or objective panel names, counts or places them. Repeat with F12 (truth view) on: the developer view shows them, the HUD still does not. Press F12 off.
21. **Camera hack.** Find the camera control terminal; the prompt list shows `Interact Camera control` with the interact prompt when in reach. Hack it. Expected: the interact entry disappears when you leave reach; hostiles in the cameras' view become observed (diamond, Target panel) as they enter it.
22. **Recon Scan.** Tab to Kestrel, arm ability 2, aim within range (also on dark ground), confirm. Expected: the slot goes on a 25 s cooldown; observed enemies inside the circle get a diamond for about 6 s, then a hollow LAST KNOWN mark; the aiming line never named an unobserved enemy.
23. **Device switching.** Without restarting, move the mouse, then press a button on an Xbox pad, then a PlayStation pad, then (if available) a Nintendo pad, then the keyboard. Expected: the prompt chips (key/button names), the pause banner prompt and the ability slot prompts change at once with each device; labels come from the bindings (Nintendo Cancel and Xbox Cancel both read `B`); no hard-coded key names appear. A generic pad shows chords for the abilities.
24. **Mouse: squad cards.** Click a card: that unit alone becomes selected. Shift+click another: it is added to (or removed from) the selection. Double-click a card: that unit takes control (same as Tab). Expected: no unit receives a move order from these clicks. Clicking a dead (`DOWN`) card does nothing (the eligibility rule is in `HudRequests`).
25. **Mouse: abilities, CLEAR, chips.** Click an ability slot: it arms; click it again: it disarms. Click CLEAR: the queue empties. Click the FOLLOW chip: follow toggles. Click the PAUSE chip: pause toggles (it reads RESUME while paused). Expected: each does exactly what its keyboard key does.
26. **Controller equivalents.** Repeat 24 and 25 with a controller: LB/RB switch control and selection; LT + Confirm on a unit adds it to the selection; RT + D-pad arms an ability (again disarms); D-pad down is Stop; D-pad up is Follow; Start / Menu / Options / + toggles pause. Expected: same results as the mouse. The tactical cursor cannot click HUD elements (known limit).
27. **Clicks do not fall through.** Click on a HUD panel (squad column, operative panel, objectives, top-right chips, prompt list, the `TACTICAL PAUSE` banner while paused, the SUCCESS / FAILED banner): no unit is ordered and no selection is lost. With the game running (banner hidden), the same spot under the banner is a normal world click. Press on a panel and drag onto the ground: no drag-select box and no order. Click the ground between panels: the selected unit moves. Right-drag the camera and use the wheel over a panel: the camera still moves (not gated, known).
28. **Regenerate mid-mission.** Press F6, F7 and F5, also while paused, also with an ability armed and a queue shown. Expected: squad cards rebuild for the new squad with no stale name or health, no stale objective rows, Target panel, queue, prompts or world marks; the pause banner state is correct; the console shows no exception.
29. **Layout at other resolutions.** At 1920x1080 nothing overlaps and the centre is empty. Resize the Game view to 1280x720, 2560x1440, 1920x1200 and 2560x1080 (21:9). Expected: panels stay anchored to their corners, no text wraps or clips, and nothing overlaps; the tightest gap is between the objectives and the squad column at 21:9.
30. **Eye check: 4:3 overlap.** Set the Game view to 1024x768 (4:3). Expected (known limit): the squad column and the operative panel overlap by about 33 canvas units. Report if it looks worse.
31. **Eye check: ability slot with a pad chord.** With a generic or PlayStation pad active, look at the ability slots. Expected: the prompt chip spans the top row and the text shrinks to fit (never below 10 px) without cutting off the ability name. Report any truncation.
32. **Eye check: objective glyphs.** Look at the `[x]`, `[ ]`, `[?]`, `[-]` and `[!]` markers in the objective panel. Expected: readable at 1920x1080 and distinguishable from each other by shape as well as colour. `[!]` and `[-]` are not drawn as icons (placeholder text).
33. **Console.** After all of the above the Console shows no errors and no HUD warnings. In Play mode the hierarchy has a single EventSystem, under `TacticalHud` (created at runtime, a sibling of `HudCanvas`); selecting a HUD button with the keyboard (arrow keys, Enter; not Tab, which switches character) does nothing: the HUD never takes focus.
34. **Intel map placement.** With fog on (the Blind preset), press M if the INTEL map is hidden. Expected: the map sits on the left, directly above the top squad card (about 12 units of gap), left edges aligned. Kill or lose a squad member and regenerate with fewer cards: the map moves down with the roster. Press F1: the map does not move. Known limit: with a long objective list on a very short canvas (21:9) the map can touch the objectives panel.
